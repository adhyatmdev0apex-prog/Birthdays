=====================================================================
  HOW TO ADD PHOTOS FOR RITIKA MA'AM'S BIRTHDAY WEBSITE
=====================================================================

The website looks complete right out of the box with warm gold-framed
placeholders, and automatically displays your real photos the moment
you drop them into the paths below:

1. Dedicated Portrait Photo (Scene 02):
   -> assets/images/portrait.jpg

2. Cherished Memories Gallery (Scene 04 — 4 polaroid frames):
   -> assets/images/memories/01.jpg
   -> assets/images/memories/02.jpg
   -> assets/images/memories/03.jpg
   -> assets/images/memories/04.jpg

Tip: Portrait (4:5) or square photos work best for the portrait &
polaroid frames. You can also edit titles, captions, wishes, and the
gratitude letter anytime in: data/teacher.js
